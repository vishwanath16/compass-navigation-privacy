# Privacy Policy — Compass Navigation

_Effective date: 18 October 2026_

Compass Navigation ("the app") is a walking compass that points at a place you choose. It is built to work almost entirely on your phone. This policy explains exactly what the app does with information.

**In short: your location never leaves your phone. The developer receives no data from the app — no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.**

## Location

- The app uses your phone's **precise location** to work out the direction and distance to your destination.
- Location is used **only while you use it**: while a compass screen is open, or while a Backtrack recording **you started** is running (shown by a "Recording your path" notification you can stop at any time).
- The app does **not** request background location access.
- Your location is **never sent** to the developer, to Google, or to anyone else.

Your position is determined by your phone's location services, provided by Google Play services. How those services handle data is governed by your device's location settings and Google's privacy policy, not by this app.

## What is stored on your phone

The app keeps the following **only on your phone**:

- **Recent destinations** — the last 10 places you opened.
- **Waypoints** — places you choose to save, with the names you give them.
- **Backtrack path** — the route you record when you start Backtrack.
- **Settings** — for example, high-contrast mode.

This data is **not backed up** to the cloud and is **not transferred** to other devices. You can delete any of it in the app (swipe to delete, or "Delete Path"), and uninstalling the app removes all of it.

If you open a destination, Android may also show it as a shortcut when you long-press the app icon. That shortcut is stored by your phone's launcher on your device.

## When the app uses the internet

The app connects to the internet in exactly one situation: **when you share or paste a Google Maps short link (for example, maps.app.goo.gl/…) or a short plus code with a town name (for example, "XFJM+HR Bengaluru")**. The app then sends **that link or text** to Google (google.com, maps.app.goo.gl) over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to find the place's coordinates.

- Your current location is **not** included.
- No cookies, advertising IDs or other identifiers are sent.
- Nothing is sent when you share a link that already contains coordinates, a geo: link, a full plus code, or typed coordinates — these are handled on your phone.
- Google's handling of that request is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Precise and approximate location | To point at your destination. Asked only when you first need it. |
| Notifications (Android 13+) | Only for the Backtrack "Recording your path" notification. Asked only when you start Backtrack. |
| Foreground service (location) | Lets Backtrack keep recording with the screen off, only after you start it. |
| Internet | Only to expand Google Maps links and look up plus codes, as described above. |
| Vibration | One short vibration when you arrive. |

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone.
